# Incident Management & Operational Runbooks

## 1. Severity Classifications
- Sev-1: Complete service outage affecting all end users
- Sev-2: Degraded performance or loss of redundancy (e.g., single AZ failure)
- Sev-3: Minor non-critical bug with viable workaround


## 2. Post-Mortem Root Cause Analysis
Blameless post-mortems are conducted within 48 hours of any Sev-1/2 event, documenting timeline, root cause, and preventative action items.
